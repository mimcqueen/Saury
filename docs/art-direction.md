# Saury! — Art Direction

Companion to [game-design.md](game-design.md). Visual north star: Mitchell’s refs under `media/refs/` (Gang Beasts barn/aquarium/elevators, party laser arena, Buckshot table, horror bathroom) — see [preferences.md](../preferences.md).

---

## Thesis

Bootleg daytime talk show trapped in a black-box theater: nicotine air, one hot spotlight, audience as **eyes only**, full **VHS** output. Friend-slop jokes live in deadbeat minis + cursed AI babies — the **frame** stays eerie and dingy, not candy Quiplash.

---

## Visual bar (from Mitchell’s refs)

| Ref energy | Steal this | Avoid this |
|---|---|---|
| **Buckshot Roulette table** | Intimate close three-quarters, cramped wood/felt, oppressive quiet between jokes, readable props under one key light | Wide empty stages, esports neon |
| **Gang Beasts barn / aquarium / elevators** | Soft mush characters, readable silhouettes in chaos, vertical play (elevators), wet/reflective comedy sets, camera that keeps all bodies in frame | Precision fighter cams, tiny hard-to-read avatars |
| **Party laser arena** | Occasional mini with kitschy lights / haze as spice — not the hub default | Making the whole game a rave |
| **Horror bathroom** | Pissing Contest set: grimy tile, flickering fluorescents, gross-cute, Host-adjacent spotlight through a doorway | Photoreal bodily fluids; anything that tanks Steam rating |

**Camera bar:** Liar’s Bar intimacy — orbit, push-in, whip-pan into arenas, smash to baby roll-out, eye cutaways. Prefer locked-off + deliberate cuts over frantic shake.

**Material bar:** Matte flats, scuffed wood, dusty velvet, plastic/latex doll skin on dads. Crushed blacks; nicotine amber; bruise mauve; sickly mint; spotlight near-white.

**Post:** Full VHS (tracking, timestamp chrome, chroma, scanlines) — subject must stay readable.

## Characters

- **6 creature bases:** Human, Goblin, Catgirl, Cryptid, Blob, Imp.  
- Hub dolls: attribute kits + mouse face morph + Smooth/Pixel.  
- Baby: AI → **3D mesh** with bowling-alley pomp (lights, chute/return energy, animatronic idle). Billboard = interim only.  
- Host: stylized NPC; mouth flaps; never a player avatar.
